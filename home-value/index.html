What's my home worth?

A real number from closed sales in your area, not a website guess. Free, no appointment, and you'll have it within a day.

Here's what I do instead: pull three to five closed sales from the last six months that actually compare to your house, adjust honestly for what's different in both directions, look at what's pending and what's sitting, and give you a range and the reasoning behind it. Over the phone, or at your kitchen table if you'd rather.

No listing appointment comes with it. If you're two years from selling and just want a number to plan around, that's a fine reason to ask.
